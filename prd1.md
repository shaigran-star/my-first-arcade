who plays:1 player
on screen:cube jumps over spikes of different sizes and spacing
win or lose:when the cube hits a spike
gameplay:player controls a cube that moves forward automatically and must jump to avoid spikes
challenge:spikes appear in single and grouped patterns, and the speed gradually increases over time
objective:survive as long as possible and score points for each spike safely passed
visual style:simple colorful side-scrolling arcade game with a sky background, ground, and clear obstacle shapes
